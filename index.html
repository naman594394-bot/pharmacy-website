About Us

Bharat Medical Store has been a cornerstone of the Hapur community since 1967. For over 45 years, families across Hapur have relied on us for their everyday medicine and healthcare needs. We take pride in knowing our customers by name and in the trust they place in us each and every day.

As a full-service retail pharmacy, we are committed to keeping our shelves well-stocked and our doors open seven days a week, 365 days a year including all public holidays. Whether you need a prescription filled, over-the-counter medicines, or health and wellness products, Bharat Medical Store is here for you.

Our Services

We are a full-service retail pharmacy serving the Hapur community. Visit us for all your everyday healthcare needs:
